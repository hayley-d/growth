## Traversal checkpoint

This quiz checks that you can tell the four traversals apart.

- **Pre-order:** node, left, right.
- **In-order:** left, node, right.
- **Post-order:** left, right, node.
- **Level-order:** one row at a time, top to bottom.

Hints lower your score. When an answer is wrong, you get a link to the lesson to review.
