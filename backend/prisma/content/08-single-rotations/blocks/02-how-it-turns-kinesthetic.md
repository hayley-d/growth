## Turn it with paper

Write 40, 20, 50, 10, 30 and 5 on scraps of paper. Lay out the tree: 40 at the top, 20 on its left and 50 on its right, 10 and 30 under 20, and 5 under 10 on the left. Now rotate right at 40. Lift 20 to the top. Move 40 down to the right of 20, keeping 50 on its right. Move 30 across so it sits on the left of 40. Read the scraps left to right. They are still 5, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50.
