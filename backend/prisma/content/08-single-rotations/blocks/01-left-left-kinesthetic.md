## Rotate it with paper

Write 30, 20 and 10 on three scraps of paper. Lay them in a stick down to the left: 30 at the top, 20 below it on the left, 10 below 20 on the left. Now do the right rotation: lift 20 up to the top, slide 30 down to the right of 20, and leave 10 where it is on the left of 20. Read the keys left to right before and after. They are still 10, 20, 30.
