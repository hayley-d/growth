## Rotate it with paper

Write 10, 20 and 30 on three scraps of paper. Lay them in a stick down to the right: 10 at the top, 20 below it on the right, 30 below 20 on the right. Now do the left rotation: lift 20 up to the top, slide 10 down to the left of 20, and leave 30 where it is on the right of 20. Read the keys left to right before and after. They are still 10, 20, 30.
