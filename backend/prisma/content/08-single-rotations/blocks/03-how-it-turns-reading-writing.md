## Write the four steps

This tree is 40 at the top, with 20 on its left. 20 has 10 on the left and 30 on the right.

```
    40
   /
  20
 /  \
10    30
```

Write the four steps of a **right rotation** at 40 in your own words.

Answer:

1. 20 moves up.
2. 40 becomes the right child of 20.
3. 30 (the inner subtree) becomes the left child of 40.
4. Heights update.

The tree is then 20 at the root, with 10 on the left and 40 on the right, and 30 under 40 on the left.
