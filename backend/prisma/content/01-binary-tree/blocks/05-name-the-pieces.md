## Name the pieces

The tree you built has a **root**, 8, at the top.

The nodes directly below a node are its **children**. The node above them is their **parent**. 4 and 12 are the children of 8, so 8 is their parent.

A node with no children is a **leaf**. This tree has four: 2, 6, 10 and 14.

In a **binary** tree every node has at most two children: a **left** child and a **right** child.
