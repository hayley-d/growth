## Subtrees

A node and everything below it is a **subtree**. The left subtree of 8 is 4, 2 and 6.

In a search tree, everything in the left subtree of a node is smaller than the node, and everything in the right subtree is bigger. So 2, 4 and 6 are all smaller than 8, and 10, 12 and 14 are all bigger.
