## Depth and height

The **depth** of a node is how many edges lie between it and the root. The root has depth 0, 4 and 12 have depth 1, and the leaves have depth 2.

The **height** of a node counts the nodes on the longest way down to a leaf, starting with the node itself. A leaf has height 1, and 4 and 12 have height 2.

The height of the tree is the height of its root: 3. That's the deepest depth plus 1.

Some books count height in edges instead, which makes a leaf height 0. In this course a leaf has height 1.
