## Spot the case from the balance factors

1. Find the unbalanced node (balance factor **+2** or **−2**).
2. Find its **taller child**: the child on the side it leans to.
3. Compare the signs of their balance factors.

**Same sign, or the child is 0:** the path is straight. One rotation, as in lesson 8.

**Opposite signs:** the path turns. Two rotations.

| Case | Unbalanced node | Taller child | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Left-left | +2 | +1 or 0 | Rotate right at the node |
| Right-right | −2 | −1 or 0 | Rotate left at the node |
| Left-right | +2 | −1 | Rotate left at the child, then right at the node |
| Right-left | −2 | +1 | Rotate right at the child, then left at the node |
