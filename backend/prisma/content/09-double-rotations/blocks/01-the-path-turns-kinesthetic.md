## Make three cards

Write `30`, `10` and `20` on three scraps of paper. Lay them on your desk in the shape of the tree:

- `30` at the top
- `10` below it, on the left
- `20` below `10`, on the right

Trace the path from `30` to `20` with your finger. Your finger has to turn.

Keep the cards. You will rotate them in the next steps.
