## Do both rotations with your cards

Start from the step 1 shape.

1. **Straighten:** rotate left at `10`. Lift `20` into the place of `10`, and put `10` below `20` on the left. The cards are now in a straight line.
2. **Fix:** rotate right at `30`. Lift `20` to the top, with `10` below it on the left and `30` below it on the right.

Press **Step** on the animation and check your cards against it.
