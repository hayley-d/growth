## Rotate your cards

Start from the step 1 shape. Do one right rotation at `30`:

1. Lift `10` up to the top.
2. Put `30` below it, on the right.
3. Move `20` across, to sit below `30` on the left.

Trace the path from `10` to `20`. It still turns, just the other way.

Put the cards back in the step 1 shape for the next step.
