## Mirror your cards

Lay your cards out as the mirror image:

- `10` at the top
- `30` below it, on the right
- `20` below `30`, on the left

1. **Straighten:** rotate right at `30`. `20` takes the place of `30`, and `30` goes below `20` on the right.
2. **Fix:** rotate left at `10`. `20` goes to the top, with `10` on its left and `30` on its right.

Press **Step** on the animation and check your cards against it.
