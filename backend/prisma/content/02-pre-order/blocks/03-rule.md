## The pre-order rule

Pre-order follows three steps, starting at the root:

1. Visit the node.
2. Do pre-order on its left subtree.
3. Do pre-order on its right subtree.

A subtree is a node and everything below it. So steps 2 and 3 use the same three steps again, on a smaller tree.
