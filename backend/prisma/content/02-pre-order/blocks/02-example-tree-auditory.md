## Hear the tree

Read the tree out loud, one level at a time: "8. Then 4 and 12. Then 2, 6, 10 and 14."

Now say the two halves: "Left of 8: 4, 2, 6. Right of 8: 12, 10, 14." You'll hear these two groups in every trace.
