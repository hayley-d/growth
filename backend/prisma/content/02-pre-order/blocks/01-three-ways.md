## Three ways to go deep

A traversal visits every node in a tree exactly once. The three **depth-first** orders go all the way down one side before they try the other.

They differ in one thing: when a node is visited compared to its two subtrees. We start with **pre-order**. Pre means before: a node is visited _before_ its children.
