## Say the rule

Pre-order has a three-beat rhythm. You can say it out loud, **me, left, right.**

Me is the node you are currently visiting. Left is the left subtree and right is the right subtree
Repete the rythm for each node you reach. 

You'll find that lines 3, 4 and 5 of the code follow the same rhythm.
