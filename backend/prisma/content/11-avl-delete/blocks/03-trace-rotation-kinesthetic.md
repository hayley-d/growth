## Walk it with your finger

Draw the tree on paper. Cross out the deleted key `20`.

Put a finger on the **parent** of the deleted key. Move it up **one parent at a time**.

At each node, work out the balance factor **before** you press **Step**. Stop at the first node where it is -2 or 2. Then look at its heavy child.
